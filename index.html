1. Purpose & Equal Educational Opportunities

This platform evaluates educational equity across multiple villages, ensuring that both boys and girls are receiving equal educational opportunities without gender disparity.

  • 📊 Analyzing enrollment distribution across communities.
  • 📈 Tracking exact gender ratios (Girls per 100 Boys) to measure participation.
  • 🎓 Promoting equal access to resources, attendance support, and retention.
